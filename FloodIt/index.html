Game Instructions

Flood It is a logic and strategy game. The goal is to flood the entire grid with a single color in the fewest moves possible.

  1. The game starts with a grid of random colors.
  2. Click one of the color buttons at the bottom to change the color of the top left corner.
  3. All adjacent cells of the same color will also change.
  4. Continue selecting colors to expand your area until the entire grid is the same color.
  5. Try to complete the game in the fewest moves possible.
  6. You can change the difficulty to face larger grids.

Good luck and have fun!
